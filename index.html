Prof. Lei Wang, Ph.D., P.E.

Dr. Wang is an Associate Professor of Civil Engineering and Director of the Sustainable and Resilient Geotechnical Infrastructure Laboratory at the University of Cincinnati. His work spans geotechnical risk and reliability, civil infrastructure resilience, underground excavation and tunneling, foundation engineering, earth dams and levees, soil/rock-structure interaction, and geotechnical earthquake engineering.

He has served as Managing Editor of Engineering Geology, Associate Editor of the ASCE Journal of Computing in Civil Engineering, and editorial board member for major geotechnical and geological engineering journals.

Research areas

The group develops innovative solutions for sustainable and resilient geotechnical infrastructure through reliability, numerical modeling, sensing, and data-informed decision analysis.

  • Infrastructure Robustness and Resilience
  • Reliability-based Geotechnical Design and LRFD Calibration
  • Geotechnical Risk and Reliability
  • Transportation Geotechnics
  • Discrete Element Modeling
  • Risk-informed Decision Analysis and Machine Learning in Geotechnical Engineering
  • Numerical and Centrifuge Modeling
  • Geotechnical Earthquake Engineering
  • Drill-Regolith Interaction
