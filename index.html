Engineering safer, smarter and more resilient infrastructure.

Prof. Lei Wang, Ph.D., P.E.

Dr. Wang is an Associate Professor of Civil Engineering and Director of the Sustainable and Resilient Geotechnical Infrastructure Laboratory at the University of Cincinnati. He has extensive research experience in geotechnical engineering and infrastructure risk and resilience, including geological hazards and landslides, geotechnical risk and reliability, risk and resilience assessment of civil infrastructure, underground excavation and tunneling, soil/rock-structure interaction, numerical and centrifuge modeling, earthen slopes and levees, in-situ characterization of extraterrestrial regolith, and sensing and machine learning in geotechnical engineering. He has published over 70 peer-reviewed papers in international journals. His research has been supported by NSF, NASA, DOT, USDA, NRC, USGS, and NIST. He was a recipient of many honors and awards, including the Best Paper Award by Taiwan Geotechnical Society and the Springer Nature Editor of Distinction Award. He has served in editorial roles for several major journals in geotechnical and geological engineering, including as Managing Editor of Engineering Geology (Elsevier), Associate Editor of the Journal of Computing in Civil Engineering (ASCE), and an Editorial Board Member of Computers and Geotechnics (Elsevier), Journal of Cold Regions Engineering (ASCE), Marine Georesources & Geotechnology (Taylor & Francis), Bulletin of Engineering Geology and the Environment (Springer Nature), and Journal on Geotechnical Engineering Research (ISSMGE).

He has served as Managing Editor of Engineering Geology, Associate Editor of the ASCE Journal of Computing in Civil Engineering, and editorial board member for major geotechnical and geological engineering journals.

Research areas

The group develops innovative solutions for resilient and sustainable geosystems and infrastructure through advanced sensing, computational and probabilistic modeling, artificial intelligence, digital twins, and risk-informed decision-making.

  • Geological Hazards and Landslides
  • InSAR and Remote Sensing
  • Geotechnical Risk and Reliability
  • Transportation Geotechnics
  • Levees and Flood Protection System
  • AI and Digital Twins in Geotechnical and Geological Engineering
  • Numerical and Centrifuge Modeling
  • Geotechnical Earthquake Engineering
  • Planetary Geotechnics & Regolith Mechanics
