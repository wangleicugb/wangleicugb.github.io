Graduate Assistantship for PhD Students in Geotechnical Engineering

Published: September 10, 2026
Category: Positions
Term: Spring & Fall 2027

Dr. Wang’s research group invites applicants for two PhD positions in geotechnical engineering for Spring & Fall 2027. Highly motivated individuals with a strong background in numerical modeling, especially discrete element method or material point method, machine learning and probabilistic methods, and non-invasive sensing methods in geotechnical engineering, adequate English proficiency, and the ability to work both independently and as part of a collaborative team are encouraged to apply.

Required qualifications

Applicants must have a B.S. degree and a master degree in one of the following areas:

  • Civil engineering
  • Geotechnical engineering
  • GIS or remote sensing

Ideal candidate background

Ideal candidates should have a strong background in one or more of the following areas:

  • Geotechnical modeling and simulation, including DEM or MPM
  • Machine learning and probabilistic methods
  • InSAR and remote sensing methods

Compensation package and contact information

The graduate assistantship will be provided to cover tuition and living expenses. Interested individuals are encouraged to send their current CV to Dr. Lei Wang at wang4li@ucmail.uc.edu.

Email subject line: “Prospective PhD student in geotechnical engineering”
